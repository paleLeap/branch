BRANCH — START HERE
===================

Branch finds people near you who need what you do, and hands you a
link to what they wrote. You decide who to contact. Branch never
messages anybody, ever.


TO START IT
-----------

Double-click  Branch.exe  in this folder.

The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC".
Click  More info  ->  Run anyway.
That warning shows up for any program that hasn't paid Microsoft for a
certificate. Nothing is wrong.

Keep this whole folder together. Branch.exe needs the files next to it.


YOUR FIRST SCAN — do exactly this
---------------------------------

1. TRADE      Click the Trade box and start typing your work.
              "plumb" finds Plumbing, "handy" finds Handyman.
              There are 101 to choose from.

2. LOCATION   It fills in your nearest city by itself. Change it if
              you work somewhere else.

3. HOW FAR    50 miles is a good start.

4. HOW RECENT "Last week" is a good start.

5. SOURCES    Tick  Reddit  and  Facebook.
              Leave the search box EMPTY the first time. Your trade
              already knows what its customers say.

6. FACEBOOK   Click the Facebook button. It will tell you it needs an
              account and offer you a sign-in page. Sign in there, in
              Branch's own window.
              Branch NEVER sees your password. Use a spare account if
              you would rather.

7. GO         Press  Go, go, go.


WHAT TO EXPECT
--------------

A scan takes a few minutes. Facebook is slow on purpose — it reads at
a human pace so nobody's account gets flagged.

The dot at the top pulses while it works. If the dot stops moving,
something is stuck; close it and start again.

You should get a handful of real leads, not hundreds. One to five good
ones is a normal scan. This finds work you would never have seen — it
is not a firehose.

Each result shows what the person wrote, where, how long ago, and WHY
it matched. Click a result to open the original post in your browser.

Nothing matched? Widen the miles, go back further, or check the
"Discarded" tab to see what was thrown out and why.


IF IT KEEPS FINDING NOTHING
---------------------------

- Make sure Facebook is ticked AND you signed in (step 6).
- Try Plumbing or Handyman — those two find the most.
- Try a bigger city nearby.


YOUR PRIVACY, AND THEIRS
------------------------

No account with us. No server. Nothing is sent anywhere.
Results live on your screen and are gone when you close it.
Branch keeps your settings and which sites you signed into —
never a password, and never a file about anyone whose post it read.
